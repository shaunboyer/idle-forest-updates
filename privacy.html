Idle Forest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Idle Forest is a free iPhone game about regrowing a forest. This policy explains what information the game collects and why. In short: we collect anonymous stats about how the game is played, and you can turn that off in Settings.

What we collect

If anonymous stats are on, the game sends the following:

  • A random install ID. The game creates this number when it's installed. It isn't linked to your name, your Apple account, or your device's advertising ID. Reinstalling the game creates a new one.
  • Play progress. For example, your player level, the trees you've planted and grown, chores finished, story moments reached, and when a play session starts and ends.
  • In-game choices. For example, the items, boosts and decorations you get with in-game currency.
  • Technical details. Your iPhone model, iOS version, and the version of the game you're using.

We use this to understand how many people play, how far they get, and where the game could be better.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. The game has no accounts, no ads, and no advertising tracking. We don't sell your information.

IP addresses

Like any internet request, sending stats exposes your device's IP address to the service receiving them. We've set our analytics service to discard it rather than store it with your stats, and the game asks it not to work out your location.

The game also checks a public file on GitHub for news of a new version. GitHub may log that request under its own privacy policy.

Your saved game

Your progress is saved on your iPhone. It isn't sent to us as a saved game. If you delete the game, that save may be deleted with it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your iPhone. They don't send your information to us.

Who handles the stats

Stats are processed and stored in the United States by PostHog, our analytics service. They're kept for up to one year.

Your choices

  • Turn stats off: on the game's home screen, open Settings and set Anonymous stats to Off. The game stops sending stats and clears any waiting to be sent.
  • Ask a question or make a request: email us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete information about you. Because the stats aren't tied to your identity, we may not be able to find which records are yours.

Children

Idle Forest doesn't ask anyone, including children, for personal information. If you're a parent or guardian and have a concern, please email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
